# Plusit 
Gestión laboral simplificada para trabajadores agrícolas de temporada.

## About the Project
Plusit is a mobile application designed specifically for seasonal workers (formal temporary workers) in the agricultural, forestry, and packing sectors in the La Araucanía region, Chile.
The project was created to solve a critical User Experience (UX) gap: the disconnect between Human Resources departments and field workers who face conditions of low digital literacy, limited hardware (low-end phones), and intermittent or no connectivity.

### Our UX Approach
Instead of adapting traditional corporate software, Plusit is built from the ground up under principles of extreme accessibility and cognitive load reduction. We transform bureaucratic procedures and HR technical jargon into everyday, visual, and stress-free interactions.

## Key Feautures (Value Proposition)
* **1. Offline Mode:** Ensures the app remains functional in rural areas with intermittent or no signal.
* **2. Fatt-Finger Friendly Design:** Large buttons and extended touch zones prevent errors for users with tired hands or damaged screens
* **3. Performance Simulator:** A visual tool to track daily yield per crate and estimate earnings in real-time.
* **4. Shift Swapping Dashboard:** A simplified system for workers to coordinate turn changes for personal or family needs
* **5. Visual Confirmation:** Clear status indicators like the "Permit Status Traffic Light" and "One-tap share with family" to simplify communication with loved ones and supervisors.
